# Hush — screen and design guide

Read with CLAUDE.md. This describes the current 0.8 look and screens. It is a guide for keeping the app visually consistent, not a limit on new features: the user's latest directions override it, and new screens/categories should be added in this same visual language. Behavior notes below describe how 0.7 works today and may change as features are added. Images in docs/References establish appearance only.

## Priorities

1. Readable text and stable geometry: no clipped labels, no hover scaling, no gauge movement when results appear.
2. Quiet hierarchy: heading/gauge, scan status, reviewed cleanup; navigation, category selection and cleanup remain separate.
3. Neutral surfaces: safety color lives in vertical lines, checkmarks, checked primary labels and thin checked-category outlines. All categories share one continuous neutral surface.
4. Gentle feedback: small under-button light, smooth shared page transitions, a live odometer file count and a distinct completed gauge.

## Setup window

`SetupWindow` (installer and uninstaller) is a 540-wide card with the app's 24 DIP rounded transparent window, drawn in the Dark palette: the "Hush" wordmark (33 Regular Italic, accent) with a Close caption button, a 26 Regular Italic heading, a 16 body paragraph that says exactly what will happen, one option checkbox (Install: "Add a shortcut to my desktop", checked; Uninstall: "Also delete my Hush settings, history and logs", unchecked, with a hint that it also removes the Undo history), a quiet text button on the left and the single action on the right (Install/Update with the normal action style; Remove Hush with the red-orange Clean style). Results replace the text in place ("Hush is installed" with Open Hush, "Hush was removed" with Close); failures show in the red-orange text and leave everything retryable.

## Top bar and Settings

- Settings, Theme, Minimize and Close share CaptionButton: 40 x 40 DIP, transparent, zero glow, 10 DIP padding, MutedBrush easing toward TextBrush with the shared 280 ms interaction timing. Related gaps are 4 DIP, with 8 DIP separating theme from window commands. Center every icon in an 18 DIP vector slot with 1.5 DIP rounded strokes. Minimize/Close ink uses a precise 10 DIP command glyph within that slot. No font-symbol icons, new borders, background wash or scaling.
- Theme shows the current mode as sunlight: Dark core/ray opacity 0.30/0.55; Light 1/1. Tooltips and automation names state the destination theme. The theme button and Appearance checkbox stay synchronized.
- Settings is three cards: Smart Boost, Privacy & telemetry (diagnostic data, personalized offers, Settings suggestions) and More impact (Location, Widgets). Every choice is an on/off switch row. The theme is changed only with the top-bar button; Windows switches only stage a reviewed batch. Back returns to its originating Home/Review page, retaining scan results, selection and expanded categories. Disable Settings during a scan/cleanup operation.
- (Replaced in 0.8: see "Icon and ice-blue accent" below.) The old executable icon was a single #3CCFD9 "H" (Inter SemiBold) on the charcoal rounded square, generated by src/Hush/Assets/GenerateIcon.ps1 at 16/24/32/48/64/128/256 px with raster ink centered within one pixel per axis.

The requested app motion runs even when Windows ClientAreaAnimation is false; never change OS settings.
- Center the visible number and its file/files label as one group within the full status width, on matching font metrics/baseline. No reserved five-digit placeholder space or off-center short totals. It is an AccentTextButton, so hover eases to AccentHoverBrush. This alone opens Review. Below: actual files/bytes and registry entries separately. Compact Clean appears only after a successful complete scan with selected candidates. Limited/stopped results remain clearly labeled partial and cannot be cleaned. - Home Clean: min-width 108, height 40, 14 SemiBold, natural width from label, nearly transparent fill, one-DIP Hush red-orange border, matching text and a restrained outer glow. No large bright filled red rectangle.

## Empty and incomplete results

- Keep one internal partition per catalogued category, but bind Review to a filtered view of nonempty categories. Expand/Collapse All operates only on those visible partitions; no empty section, divider or no-files row is rendered.
- A completed empty scan replaces CategorySurface with a spacious centered message: Everything looks clean. Use 22 SemiBold HeadingBrush and a 14 Medium MutedBrush detail, 22 DIP line height with a 12 DIP gap, max-width 440 and 28 DIP margin. Hide selection controls, expansion controls, selected totals and the redundant zero-result header detail. Keep Back and one neutral Scan again in their usual positions.
- If locations were inaccessible or browsers were skipped, say No cleanup items found and explain that some locations were skipped. An incomplete empty scan says No results to review; a failure says The scan couldn't finish. Neither claims the system is clean.
- Registry-only results are nonempty and remain opt-in. Removing the final remaining candidate restores the empty state. A stopped or limited scan can be reviewed read-only: clear default candidate selections, disable file/category checkboxes, replace the global checkbox with Partial results - read-only, and replace selected totals with Read-only results. Keep expansion and one rescan available. Do not offer selections that cannot enable Clean. Stopping during final gauge/counter settlement cancels success eligibility and completion glow; the gauge remains below 100%.
- Preserve existing gauge, odometer and selection motion. Visibility changes use existing reserved Home slots and the shared incoming-page fade; no gauge movement or decorative animation is introduced.

About opens as a compact 500-wide card, approximately 247 high, padding 32 and radius 24. Only essential copy: product heading 28 Regular Italic in the primary Cyan (the wordmark style), actual version 16 Medium (8 DIP gap), one short description 17 Medium / 26 line-height (20 DIP gap): "A portable Windows cleaner. Review before you clean." No platform/channel suffix, safety legend or technical paragraphs. Release notes (quiet secondary, left), Check for updates (primary Cyan AccentTextButton, centered in the open space with 16 DIP hit padding) and Close (right) share the footer after 28 DIP of space, using shared 44-high/16 SemiBold text controls without glow or backgrounds. Release details are closed on every About opening. They use the shared 320/280 ms height/opacity accordion with disabled/hidden content when collapsed; Check also opens them. Status/support is 15 Medium with 22/23 line-height; notes heading is 16 SemiBold. No extra colored panels, borders or glow.

No token field, no sign-in and no "GitHub" wording anywhere in the update flow: people who have never heard of GitHub can update. Checks and downloads are anonymous requests to the public release page (no Authorization header is ever sent; a test asserts it). Unavailable or unpublished releases are explained in plain words and must never say up to date. Reopened About ignores late results from closed sessions. This only works while the release page is public (see docs/RELEASING.md).

Checking shows a 4 DIP track with a soft Cyan glow gliding across it (1700 ms, sine ease-in-out, edges fade to transparent; a WPF transform animation on the render thread, not a repaint timer). Downloading turns the same track into a determinate bar that eases (240 ms) to the real download fraction beside "Downloading the update · 40%". No scale/glow; a Cancel text action while busy. Stop activity on every settled/error/canceled/closed state. New releases show their version, plain notes and one sentence saying what will happen before Download & install. Download & install is a single decision: it downloads with a real progress bar, verifies size, SHA-256 and Windows x64 identity/version, then hands over without a second confirmation. The handoff says Restarting to finish the update; installed success belongs only after the helper's startup receipt, with existing rollback protections. Failed download/verification/handoff never claims installed success.

## Global UI rules

- **Checkbox**: 20 DIP slot, 6 DIP radius, 1.5 DIP border. Unchecked is neutral (FaintBrush; MutedBrush ring eases in on hover/focus). Checked shows a ring, a vector tick (1.9 DIP round caps) and the mixed dash all in the same accent brush as the checked label, with no fill. Every ring layer shares identical geometry with layout rounding off, so edges are smooth and never double-outlined.
- **Hover** never scales or resizes. Text controls: color eases over 280 ms (TextButton Muted to Text, AccentTextButton Cyan to AccentHoverBrush, category chevron Muted to Text). Glowing buttons: existing glow gains 0.035. Checkboxes: neutral ring. Scrollbar thumb: opacity 0.45 to 0.75.
- **Scrolling**: page-level scrollers use `PageScroll`: a 10 DIP overlay bar (6 DIP thumb, 4 DIP from the edge; the width is set explicitly because the style Width alone rendered a 17 DIP system bar) overlays the right edge with no width shift, and 16 DIP end padding keeps the last card whole. Never reserve a bigger gutter on one side to clear the bar: Review has equal 22 DIP gutters and the bar sits inside the right one; Settings extends its scroller 22 DIP into the page margin (Margin -22, Padding 22) so the bar sits beside the cards. Settings cards are 16 DIP apart.

Default window 920 × 820, minimum 800 × 740. Header 68; content margins 30 sides, 8 top, 24 bottom. Window radius 24, button 13, shared category surface 14, file 10. Icon-to-label gaps 10–12; related controls 12–16; distinct groups 24–28. Only checkbox, clean-action and checked-category outlines are allowed.

## Shared motion and lighting

Theme and page transitions have separate ownership. ThemePalette.cs animates live shared brushes over 320 ms Cubic EaseOut in both directions, from their displayed color to the exact destination. Startup uses its saved palette without a transition. Rapid reversals continue from the currently displayed colors; no snapshot, whole-app opacity fade or outgoing page participates. Motion.cs follows palette destinations for button/selection ink, avoids restarting unchanged targets each frame and commits exact final colors under a completion guard. Page navigation continues to remove old content immediately and fade only the incoming page. Fixing either behavior must preserve the other's rendered regression.

- Never scale text/controls on hover or press. Gauge content never dims with focus, activation or background clicks.
- Button height 44, type 16 SemiBold, both-axis centered content; natural widths. Home Clean 40 high / 14 is the compact exception. Vector Back/chevrons use proportional 1.5–1.8 DIP strokes.
- Normal actions use a faint bottom underlay: opacity 0.12, blur 10 idle / 12 hover, shadow depth 2 downwards, accent-matched glow opacity 0.08 normal / 0.06 toggle. Hover adds 0.035, press 0.02, eased 280 ms. Outline-glow buttons (Clean, Clean selected) get a clearly visible lift in their own color: +0.11 (press +0.05) and the outer halo widens from 12 to 18 blur. Fully transparent wordmark/caption/results-link/Back controls have zero visible underlay.
- Clean actions preserve their existing geometry, nearly transparent fill (#08FF795C) and one-DIP outline. Use Hush's archived Scan again accent #FF795C, with darker #B54428 text/outline in Light. Replace the bottom underlay with centered outer-only light: two rounded silhouettes behind the surface, 4/12 DIP blur, zero shadow depth, opacity 0.221/0.13 idle. Exclude the rounded button interior from the effect container so neither label nor fill is washed out. Clip refreshes on resize. Hover/press use the shared 280 ms easing and no scaling. Caution shares the Hush red-orange family while retaining its written safety label and opt-in behavior.
- Category/file selection text and category outlines ease color over 280 ms, retargeting the displayed color; checkbox check/fill/frame/mixed layers share 280 ms Cubic EaseOut. Selection never shifts layout, scales controls or adds glow. Category checked outlines are a full 1 DIP stroke (0.5 DIP rendered as a ragged hairline at the rounded corners); verify corners in a zoomed render.
- Button fills, navigation ink, lighting and disabled opacity share Motion.InteractionMilliseconds=280 and Cubic EaseOut, retargeting from displayed values. Every page direction hides the old page immediately and fades in only the new page over 320 ms. Never retain a screenshot or render the outgoing page over the incoming one; the user rejected that ghost-image effect. Rapid navigation cancels the previous incoming fade, and a version guard prevents an old completion from clearing a newer transition. Theme resources ease live over 320 ms in both directions, preserving displayed-color continuity without a screenshot. Popups reveal over 320 ms, close 160 ms. Accordion height/opacity 320/280, category arrow 280, expand-all icon 240 ms. Completion link/Clean fade within reserved space over 500 ms.
- Gauge stages ease 950 ms; final sweep 1200–2400. At true completion, glow 0.22 → 0.72 at 650 ms → 0.50 at 1800 ms, local blur 12 → 20 → 16. Hold the settled glow until reset. No recurring pulse or fake timer-based scan progress.

## Verification

Run scripts/Verify.ps1 after UI changes. It renders both themes, checks theme/page motion, the scan-to-Clean selection workflow (on synthetic fixtures), updater and Windows settings flows (synthetic registry roots). Never clean real user files or run a real self-update to test.
